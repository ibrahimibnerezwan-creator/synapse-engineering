# Synapse Engineering & Supply — Product Truth

## 1. Core Mission & Identity
Synapse Engineering & Supply bridges the gap between China's massive manufacturing hubs (Guangdong, Shenzhen, Ningbo, Xiamen) and buyers in Bangladesh. Unlike impersonal marketplace aggregators (Alibaba/AliExpress), Synapse is anchored by **personal on-ground relationships**: a dedicated partner in China who personally visits factories, negotiates manufacturer rates, conducts video quality inspections, and coordinates turnkey air & sea delivery to Dhaka and all industrial hubs in Bangladesh.

## 2. Dual Target Audiences
1. **B2B Industrial & Enterprise Clients**:
   - Factory engineers, procurement officers, textile/spinning mills, ceramics, packaging plants.
   - Needs: Genuine Siemens SIMATIC PLCs & I/O modules, Schneider contactors/VFDs, HiTHIUM 11,000-cycle LiFePO₄ energy storage systems, Deye hybrid inverters, and obsolete/hard-to-find machinery spares.
2. **Consumers & Tech Enthusiasts**:
   - Everyday buyers looking for premium tech gadgets, smart home devices, high-capacity GaN chargers, portable power stations, and consumer electronics at direct-from-China factory prices with local warranty support.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Human & Approachable**: "Your trusted personal connection on the factory floor in Shenzhen."
- **Technically Competent**: Accurate engineering specs, clear wiring/battery longevity math, zero fluff.
- **Transparent & Trustworthy**: Direct WhatsApp hotlines, live video inspection proof, verified genuine parts.
- **Anti-AI Tells**: No fake military HUD aesthetics, no generic purple gradients, no robotic corporate jargon.
