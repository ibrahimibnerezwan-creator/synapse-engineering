Inspired by best-in-class industrial tech platforms (Anduril, Linear, Tesla Megapack, Keyence, Flexport, and Siemens Next), this document establishes four distinct, production-grade visual design directions for Synapse Engineering & Supply.

## 1. Candidate 1: "Cyber-Industrial Monolith" (Recommended)
* **Design Philosophy**: Positions Synapse Engineering not as a generic local retailer, but as an advanced industrial hardware contractor with deep technical mastery.
* **Tokens**:
  * `--bg`: `#06080c` (near-black carbon)
  * `--panel`: `#0b0f17` (obsidian HUD panel)
  * `--border`: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)` (laser cyan hair-line)
  * `--accent-cyan`: `#00f0ff` (status indicator & focus)
  * `--accent-amber`: `#ffaa00` (part model callout)
  * `--accent-emerald`: `#00ff88` (live inventory & verified QC)
* **Signature Micro-Interactions**:
  * Bracketed HUD tags: `[SYS.6ES7532]`, `LOC: GZ-CAN`
  * Tabular monospace numerals with glowing status dots
  * Terminal-style command search bar

---

## 2. Candidate 2: "Solar Kinetic Precision"
* **Design Philosophy**: Emphasizes clean energy storage, battery longevity, and ROI.
* **Tokens**:
  * `--bg`: `#040914` (deep space navy)
  * `--solar-gold`: `#f59e0b`
  * `--cyan-glow`: `#0ea5e9`
* **Signature Micro-Interactions**:
  * Kinetic energy flow lines connecting Solar Panels $\rightarrow$ Inverter $\rightarrow$ HiTHIUM LiFePO₄ $\rightarrow$ Factory Load
  * Watt-hour interactive battery sizing cards

---

## 3. Candidate 3: "Global Trade Terminal"
* **Design Philosophy**: Maximizes personal sourcing trust for factory owners looking to procure directly from China.
* **Tokens**:
  * `--bg`: `#090e17`
  * `--emerald`: `#10b981` (customs & dispatch clear)
  * `--sky`: `#38bdf8`
* **Signature Micro-Interactions**:
  * 4-step procurement timeline with live GPS coordinates (Shenzhen Lab $\rightarrow$ Ningbo $\rightarrow$ Chittagong $\rightarrow$ Dhaka)

---

## 4. Candidate 4: "Swiss Precision Industrial"
* **Design Philosophy**: Clinical, understated, authoritative corporate engineering.
* **Tokens**:
  * `--bg`: `#090a0f`
  * `--surface`: `#10121a`
  * `--accent`: `#0080ff`
